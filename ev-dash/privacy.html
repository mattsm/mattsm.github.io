EV DASH

Privacy policy

Effective November 8, 2026

EV Dash is an Android and Android Auto app made by M4 Embedded, LLC. This policy covers what the app does with data.

Short version

EV Dash does not collect, send or sell any data. Everything it reads stays on your phone.

What the app reads

  • Vehicle data from your OBD adapter, such as state of charge, battery and charging values, speed, odometer, tire pressures and the VIN (used to identify the model).
  • Car data shared through Android Auto, such as state of charge, range, speed and odometer, when EV Dash is open on the car screen.

This data is shown in the app and saved on your phone as drive, charge and log history. It is never uploaded. EV Dash has no account, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): to find and connect to your OBD adapter. EV Dash does not use Bluetooth to determine your location.
  • Network: only to talk to a Wi-Fi OBD adapter on the local network. EV Dash makes no internet connections.
  • Notifications: for the connection status and the optional unplug reminder.
  • Car data: to read the values listed above through Android Auto or Android Automotive.

Sharing

Data leaves your phone only when you choose to share a log or a diagnostics file through Android's share sheet, and it goes only where you send it.

Deleting data

You can delete history in the app. Uninstalling EV Dash, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes everything it saved.

Children

EV Dash is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
