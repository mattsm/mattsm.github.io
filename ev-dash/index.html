EV Dash

Live battery, charging and drive data from your EV, on your phone and on the car screen.

What it shows

  • State of charge, pack power, energy left and a range estimate
  • Battery health, every cell voltage, temperatures and the 12 V battery
  • Charging sessions with live power, history and charge curves
  • Drives logged to History, with CSV export
  • A tile dashboard on the car screen through Android Auto

Cars

Hyundai Ioniq 5, Ioniq 6 and Ioniq 9, Kia EV6, EV9 and EV3, and Genesis GV60. EV Dash reads the car through an ELM327-compatible OBD adapter (Bluetooth LE, Bluetooth or Wi-Fi), such as the OBDLink CX.

Read-only

EV Dash only reads data. It never sends commands that change anything in the car.

Availability

EV Dash is in private testing on Android and Android Auto.
